What We Do

One consultancy, every stage of the works.

From the first borehole on site to the final snag list, our engineers, procurement desk and site teams work as a single accountable unit.

01

Structural & Civil Engineering

Design, analysis and detailing for buildings and structural works of every description, delivered by registered civil and structural engineers.

02

Soil Investigation & Site Analysis

Analytical site reports and soil investigation ahead of design, so foundations are specified against real ground conditions, not assumptions.

03

Construction & General Contracting

Planning, erection, alteration and repair of buildings and structures, handled directly by our own contracting arm.

04

Building Materials & Procurement

Sourcing, bidding, tendering and supply of cement, blocks, bricks and construction materials — wholesale or retail — with logistics handled in-house.

05

Mechanical & Electrical Works

M&E installation and maintenance works delivered alongside the civil scope, coordinated under one project programme.

06

Haulage, Logistics & Distribution

Fleet-backed haulage and logistics, plus distribution of LPG, petroleum products and general merchandise to site.

How We Work

Four stages, one point of contact.

01

Consultation & Site Assessment

We meet, walk the site, and scope the works and including soil investigation where the project calls for it.

02

Design & Analytical Report

Structural design, drawings and an analytical site report are prepared and reviewed with you before mobilisation.

03

Procurement & Mobilisation

Materials are sourced and tendered, logistics arranged, and the site team mobilised against an agreed programme.

04

Construction & Handover

Site supervision runs through to completion, with sign-off and handover documentation provided at close-out.

Home / Services

Every service written into our Memorandum of Association are offered as one continuous scope of work.

Rather than pass a project between separate specialists, Frankfarat carries it through engineering, procurement, construction and logistics under a single contract. Below is the full breadth of what we do, organised the way a project actually moves.

A

Engineering Consultancy & Structural Design

We practise as general engineers and as civil, electrical, mechanical and structural engineers, acting as consultants and advisers to both public and private clients. Whether the brief is a full structural design from foundation to roof, a review of drawings prepared elsewhere, or specialist advice on a single element of a build, our engineers carry professional responsibility for the calculations behind it.

This is the stage where risk is designed out before it reaches site such as load paths, material specification, and buildability are worked through on paper first.

Structural design & detailing Civil engineering consultancy Electrical & mechanical engineering Design review & advisory
B

Soil Investigation & Analytical Site Reporting

Before a foundation is sized, we investigate what it will actually sit on. Our soil investigation and analytical site reporting services give designers and clients real ground data i.e bearing capacity, soil composition and site conditions rather than a generic assumption carried over from another project.

An analytical site report from Frankfarat becomes part of the permanent project record, referenced through design, construction and, where needed, dispute resolution.

Soil investigation reports Analytical site reports Ground condition assessment
C

Construction & General Contracting

As general contractors, we plan, design, erect, improve, repair, alter and where required we demolish buildings and structures of every description. This covers new-build construction of buildings, roads, bridges, drainage and public utility works, through to the renovation and rehabilitation of existing structures.

Site supervision runs throughout, with our own engineers on the ground rather than a subcontracted crew reporting up through a third party.

Building construction Roads, drainage & public works Renovation & rehabilitation Site supervision Piping, insulation & precast units
D

Procurement, Bidding & Materials Supply

We source, bid for, tender and supply building and construction materials such as cement, blocks and bricks among them such as wholesalers and retailers, alongside broader engineering and construction materials required to keep a programme moving. Where a client is running their own tender, our procurement desk can bid competitively; where we are contracted directly, materials are drawn straight from our own supply chain.

Maintenance and repair services follow the same procurement discipline once a structure is handed over.

Cement, blocks & bricks Construction materials sourcing Bidding & tendering Maintenance & repair supply
E

Mechanical & Electrical Works

Mechanical and electrical scopes are coordinated alongside the civil works rather than bolted on afterwards thereby reducing the clashes and delays that come from managing M&E as a separate contract.

Electrical installation Mechanical works Coordinated M&E programming
F

Infrastructure & Utility Works

Our contracting scope extends to infrastructure beyond individual buildings roads, bridges, waterways, wharves, marine works, railways, highways, manufacturing facilities, power plants, communication projects, drainage and sewage, and public utility works generally. We also lay infrastructure and install rigs where a project requires it, including wells, pipelines and related works.

Roads & highways Drainage & sewage works Power & communication infrastructure Pipeline & well works
G

Haulage, Logistics & Distribution

We move what a site needs, when it needs it haulage and logistics for construction materials and equipment, and distribution of liquefied petroleum gas (LPG), petroleum products, and carbonated and non-carbonated beverages and general merchandise as a separate line of business.

Haulage & logistics LPG & petroleum distribution General merchandise supply
H

Property Development & Estate Sales

Beyond construction for clients, we also act as developers acquiring, building, marketing and selling landed property, Undertake the purchase, lease or hire of construction equipment and machinery, including on behalf of other developers.

Estate development Property sales Plant & machinery hire

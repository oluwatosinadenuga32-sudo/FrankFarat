Our Approach

How a project actually moves through Frankfarat.

The same four stages apply whether the job is a single-storey repair or a multi-block estate only the intensity of each stage changes.

01

Scoping & Site Walk

We visit, measure, and discuss intent budget range, timeline, and any existing drawings or reports you hold.

02

Investigation & Design

Soil investigation where relevant, followed by structural design and an analytical site report you can review before we price the works.

03

Procurement & Programme

Materials are tendered or drawn from our own supply chain, and a construction programme is agreed and shared before mobilisation.

04

Construction & Supervision

Our own site supervision runs daily, with progress and safety checks recorded against the agreed programme.

05

Handover & Aftercare

Formal handover documentation is issued, with maintenance and repair support available afterward.

Built as one firm, so nothing gets lost between the drawing and the site.

Frankfarat Global Concept Limited was incorporated in Lagos to close the gap between engineering consultancy and on-site delivery, holding both the design table and the site office.

Company Profile

Who we are.

Frankfarat Global Concept Limited is a private company limited by shares, incorporated on 21 September 2021 under the Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020 (RC 1843403). We operate as civil engineering consultants and general contractors out of Ikeja, Lagos State, serving clients across Lagos and beyond.

Our objects, as set out in our Memorandum of Association, span structural, civil, electrical and mechanical engineering; the supply and distribution of building materials and cement; construction contracting; soil investigation and analytical site reporting; haulage and logistics; and the distribution of LPG and petroleum products. In practice, that breadth means a client rarely needs a second contractor once Frankfarat is engaged.

What Sets Us Apart
  • 01One accountable team.
    Design, procurement and site supervision sit under one roof, so responsibility never gets split between separate firms.
  • 02Materials supply in-house.
    Our procurement arm sources cement, blocks and building materials directly, keeping site programmes on schedule.
  • 03Documented from day one.
    Every engagement runs on a written scope, analytical site reporting where required, and formal handover records.
Mission

To deliver structurally sound, well-documented construction and engineering works that clients can rely on without needing to manage multiple contractors.

Vision

To grow into one of most trusted names in civil engineering consultancy and general contracting known as much for paperwork clients can trust as for buildings that stand in Nigeria.
